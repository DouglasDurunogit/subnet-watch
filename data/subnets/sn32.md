# About

It's AI is an ai-detector tool, which helps people to identify whether the text was human-written or ai-generated. In addition to just classifying texts, It's AI also provides a deep scan functionality, which shows probabilities of AI for each sentence in the text.

While ChatGpt alone has more than 1.8 billion monthly visits, we believe that the ability to accurately determine AI-generated text is really important and will become even more demanded in the future.

## Key Highlights (Full)
### Most accurate AI detector
According to the MGTD benchmark [(ICAIE 2025)](https://publications.waset.org/10014332/towards-reliable-machine-generated-text-detection-a-unified-benchmarking-framework) – currently the largest and most robust public benchmark for AI detectors (15 datasets, ~2M samples) – It’s AI ranks **#1** with over **92% ROC-AUC**, ahead of detectors like GPTZero, Originality and CopyLeaks.


On the RAID benchmark [(ACL paper 2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07940) we achieved **98.3% accuracy** on the official [leaderboard](https://raid-bench.xyz/leaderboard),  beating the biggest commercial competitor GPTZero on 8 out of 11 considered LLMs.

We also evaluated It's AI on GRiD, HC3 and GhostBuster datasets and reached on average **99.1% accuracy** and **96% F1-score**, becoming the new SOTA solution among the detectors covered in these works.

In education-related settings, It’s AI showed **0.8% false positive rate** on the ASAP 2.0 student essays dataset, and for Arabic-language benchmarks (ASJP, XL-SUM) achieved **98.7% accuracy** with **≤0.5% FPR**, demonstrating reliable performance beyond English.

You can find the full benchmarks report here: https://its-ai.org/en/accuracy
